## Information We Process

- Community posts and images submitted by users.
- Technical logs, security events, and diagnostics needed to operate and protect the service.

## How We Use Information

We use information to authenticate users, personalize sports content, provide event and venue bookings, issue tickets, support community features, prevent fraud, troubleshoot problems, and comply with legal obligations.

## Service Providers

Athzy uses service providers including Supabase for authentication, PostgreSQL hosting for application data, AWS S3 and CloudFront for image storage and delivery, and application hosting providers. These providers process data under their own security and privacy terms.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers, event or venue operators when necessary to fulfill a booking, authorities when legally required, or as part of a business transfer subject to applicable law.
